# Surface brief: Home (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the portfolio home/landing page, and the shared chrome (header, footer,
buttons, section headings) it sets the tone for. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: mixed recruiters + engineering leads scanning fast. Job: understand
Alan's full-stack enterprise fit in seconds and act. Primary action: contact
(email / LinkedIn), with secondary View Projects and Download CV.

Content: real resume data in src/data/* and src/config/site.ts. Projects are
placeholder case studies — never presented as shipped products. Employment
"May 2025–Present" wording left untouched pending owner confirmation.

Direction: user-pinned reference (teddyyee-portfolio.vercel.app — dark navy/blue
glassmorphism, violet accent, Inter + mono, pills/cards/icon-rows/skill-meters)
fused with a user-requested starlight / night-sky background. Pinned direction
beats the roll.

Memorable moment: the tech stack rendered as a faint constellation of connected
system icons over a drifting starfield; the active constellation lights on scroll.

Unresolved: whether inner pages (about/experience/projects/contact/cv) adopt the
full starfield or a calmer variant — decide during extension.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Starlight Stack. A full-stack engineer's portfolio as a quiet night sky:
real enterprise work floats as blue-glass instrument panels over a deep indigo
starfield, the tech stack drawn as a constellation of connected systems. It
refuses both the flat white resume page and the generic dark-hero-with-gradient
dev template.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo/navy night ground (#0B1026 → #0A0F22) carrying a real
starfield with faint constellation lines. Translucent blue-glass panels
(navy/50 fill, 1px blue-400/20 border, soft backlit glow — glass is the pinned
world's material, not decoration). Electric blue (#7FB2FF / blue-300) primary
accent, violet (#C4B5FD) secondary, gray-300 body on dark. Inter for display and
body; a self-hosted mono (Geist/JetBrains Mono) for tags, coordinates, and
status lines (data/labels, never a "technical" costume). Rounded-2xl glass
cards, rounded-full pills and circular icon wells. No eyebrow/kicker labels.

STORY: A mixed recruiter/engineer visitor lands, immediately reads "Alan Man —
Full-Stack Developer, enterprise web," sees the stack constellation prove range,
scans real experience as lit panels, and takes an action (Get in touch / View
Projects / Download CV).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed indigo starfield. Left column: name in large Inter,
role subtitle, one-line summary, a mono status line, and a primary "Get in touch"
button beside secondary "View Projects" / "Download CV". Right: the real portrait
in a glowing blue-glass frame ringed by a small constellation of core tech icons
joined by faint lines. A translucent glass nav bar spans the top. The primary
action is unmistakable above the fold; a steady vertical rhythm starts the next
section below.

FORM: Pinned reference world (dark-blue glass) fused with a starlight/celestial
background; direction pinned by the user, beats the roll; seed key e6e59028.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
